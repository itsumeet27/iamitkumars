About the Researcher

Dr. Amit Kumar Sharma

Materials Chemist

I am a Materials Chemist with a strong educational background in Biotechnology and a PhD in Chemistry (Analytical). My research focuses on the development, characterization and customization of inorganic nanomaterials.

My work is driven by a passion to create materials that can address real-world challenges, with a focus on Energy, Emissions, Materials Engineering and Wearable Technology.
